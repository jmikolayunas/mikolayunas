Your story, handcrafted.

Every commission begins with place, memory, and story. Together, we'll transform the geography that matters most to you into an heirloom artwork —crafted with precision, finished by hand, and built to last generations.

Understanding the Investment

These aren't mass-produced decorations—they're one-of-one collaborations between you, topographic data, and artisan craft. Each piece requires:

  • Custom geospatial data acquisition, cleaning, and processing
  • High-resolution terrain modeling and sculpting
  • Hand-selected domestic hardwoods from sustainable sources
  • Precision CNC fabrication (12-36 hours of machine time)
  • Custom vector overlays (trails, coastlines, coordinates, labels)
  • Hand-finishing, resin work, detail inlay
  • Artist framing, spline joinery, and custom-engraved nameplate
  • Progress photos & documentation throughout the build
  • Professional crating and specialist courier delivery
  • Care guidance and post-delivery support

Investment Range: Commissions begin at $4,500 and scale with size, complexity, materials, and finish level. Every commission is delivered with an artist-made frame and a custom-engraved nameplate.

Small (18" × 24"): typically $4,500–$6,500
Signature (approx. 24" × 36" to 30" × 40"): typically $6,500–$9,500
Statement (36" × 48"): typically $8,500–$14,000
Installation (48"+ / multi-panel): from $15,000+

Exact pricing is determined during consultation based on your specific vision, materials, and complexity. I'll provide a detailed quote before any commitment.

Timeline & Process

01

Consultation (Week 1)

We begin with a video call to discuss place, story, and vision. Material samples are shared, the process is explained, and a detailed quote and timeline are provided.

02

Design & Approval (Weeks 2-3)

Digital mockups are created using topographic data, with material options and finishing details included. The design is approved before fabrication begins. A 50% non-refundable deposit is due at this stage.

03

Fabrication (Weeks 4-10)

Progress photos and videos are shared as the piece comes to life—CNC carving, resin pours, and hand-finishing. This is where data meets hand-crafting.

04

Delivery (Weeks 11-12)

Your piece is professionally crated and shipped via specialist carrier, or available for local pickup. Final 50% payment due before shipping. Installation guidance included.

Total Timeline: Most commissions are delivered within 8–12 weeks from consultation. Priority scheduling is limited and can be discussed during your consult.

Common Questions

Can you create a map of anywhere in the world?

Yes. Work is based on USGS, NOAA, and international topographic datasets. Whether it’s a mountain in Nepal, a coastline in New Zealand, or a city in Europe - even the Moon or Mars —if elevation data exists, it can be crafted.

What if I'm not sure about size or budget?

That’s completely normal—it's why the process begins with a consultation. Examples at different scales are shared to help clarify how sizing impacts investment, and the right fit is found through the conversation.

Do you take commissions year-round?

Commissions are typically booked 8–12 weeks out, with a limited number accepted at a time to maintain quality. If the calendar is full, clients are added to a waitlist and notified when a slot opens.

Can I visit the studio to see work in person?

Absolutely. Studio visits are welcomed by appointment. It’s the best way to see materials, feel the craftsmanship, and get a sense of the process. Just mention it in the inquiry.
