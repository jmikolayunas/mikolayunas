Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026

What this site collects

When you submit a commission inquiry through this site, your name, email address, and the details you provide are sent directly to Jonas Mikolayunas via Formspree, a third-party form service. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and to discuss a potential commission. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses Vercel Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to collect anonymous, privacy-friendly data about site visits. No cookies are set, no personal data is collected, and no information is shared with advertisers or third parties. Data collected includes page views, referral sources, device types, and Core Web Vitals performance metrics.

Third-party services

Form submissions are processed by Formspree, whose privacy policy governs how submitted data is handled on their end. This site is hosted via Vercel, whose infrastructure may log standard server request data such as IP addresses as part of normal hosting operations.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to jonas@mikolayunas.com.
