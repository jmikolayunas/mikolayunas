About

Where geography, material, and memory take form.

The work begins with a question of motion. A coastline, a mountain range, a stretch of seafloor may read as fixed, but none of it is finished. Every landform is mid-process, shaped by forces still active, moving toward a future state on a timescale too slow to observe directly.

Elevation and depth data reconstruct a place's geologic history with precision. Wood, resin, and hand-finishing hold that history still, briefly, in a single object: evidence of where a place has been, and where it is heading.

Each commission begins with a place that matters to someone. Research into how that place was formed, its present relationship with the sea or land around it, and its likely future gives the work its structure. The story arrives afterward, carried by the collector.

The material and subject continue to change as the inquiry deepens. Wood and resin are the current vocabulary for a study that began in motion and geologic time, not its final form.

Approach

Place as Identity

Geography isn’t just scenery. It’s where memory lives. The landscapes that shape us deserve to be held as art, never reduced to souvenirs or screens.

One-of-One

Every piece is made individually for one client, from one place. Each commission is approached with equal care for the geography itself and the craft required to honor it. The relationship is personal and deliberate.

Data Meets Artistry

The work begins with scientific precision (real elevation data, accurate contours) and is finished by hand. Precision carving preserves fidelity; wood selection, resin color, and finishing bring warmth and presence.

Built to Last

These pieces are built to outlast trends, withstand moves, and hold meaning for decades. Premium materials and time-tested joinery ensure structural stability and longevity. This is craft made with permanence in mind.
