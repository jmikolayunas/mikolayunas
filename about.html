About the Studio

Where geography, craft, and human connection converge.

The Story Behind the Work

Bonas Studio began with a simple question: How do we hold on to the places that shape us?

I've always been drawn to the seemingly disparate worlds of engineering and art. Turning the digital tangible the tactile—to wood grain, resin pour, the sound of a CNC router carving elevation data into solid material. Maps on screens felt ephemeral. I wanted to create something you could touch, immerse yourself in, share stories with, and pass down.

What started as personal experiments—gifts for friends returning to hometowns, wedding presents marking where couples met—became something larger. Clients didn't just want accurate topography; they wanted their story embedded in it. The summit they climbed. The coastal cliff where they wed. The valley where they built a life.

Today, Bonas Studio operates at the intersection of precision and poetry. Every piece begins with real geospatial data—USGS elevation models, NOAA bathymetry, LiDAR scans —but the magic happens in collaboration. You bring the place. I bring the craft. Together, we create something tangible that honors both.

Our Philosophy

Place as Identity

Geography isn't just scenery—it's where memory lives. We believe the landscapes that shape us deserve to be honored as art, not reduced to souvenir trinkets or digital pixels.

Collaboration Over Consumption

You're not ordering from a catalog. You're co-creating with someone who cares as much about your story as the craft itself. Every commission is a conversation, not a transaction.

Data Meets Artistry

We start with scientific precision—real elevation data, accurate contours—but finish with human hands. CNC technology gives us accuracy; wood selection, resin color, and finishing give us soul.

Heirloom Quality

These aren't decorations. They're legacy pieces built to outlast trends, survive moves, and carry meaning across generations. We use archive-quality materials and time-tested joinery.

What We Stand For

Sustainable Sourcing

We source domestic hardwoods from certified sustainable forestry operations and salvage yards. Every piece of walnut, cherry, or maple has a story before it becomes yours.

Transparent Process

From initial consultation to final crating, you're part of the journey. We share progress photos, involve you in material selection, and never hide behind "proprietary methods."

No Mass Production

Bonas Studio will never scale into a factory. Each commission is limited by how many pieces one person can craft with full attention. Quality over quantity, always.

Lifetime Support

Every piece comes with care instructions and my direct contact. If something happens—a move, an accident, climate damage—we'll repair or restore it. These are meant to last.

Let's Create Together

Every commission begins with a conversation about place, memory, and meaning. Share your story, and we'll craft something worthy of it.
