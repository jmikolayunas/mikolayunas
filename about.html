About the Studio

Where geography, craft, and story converge.

The Story Behind the Work

Bonas Studio began with a simple question: How can we capture the places that shape us?

I’ve always been drawn to the meeting point between engineering and art—where digital precision becomes something tactile. Maps on screens can feel ephemeral. I wanted to make geography you can hold, live with, and pass on: wood grain, carved relief, and the quiet depth of resin catching light across a surface.

What started as personal experiments—gifts for friends returning to hometowns, wedding presents marking where couples met—became something larger. Clients didn't just want accurate topography; they wanted their story embedded in it. The summit they climbed. The coastal cliff where they wed. The valley where they built a life.

Today, Bonas Studio operates at the intersection of precision and poetry. Each piece begins with real geospatial data, refined into sculptural relief —but the work comes alive in collaboration. You bring the place. I bring the craft. Together, we create a work that honors both.

Studio Philosophy

Place as Identity

Geography isn’t just scenery—it’s where memory lives. The landscapes that shape us deserve to be held as art, never reduced to souvenirs or screens.

Collaboration Over Consumption

This isn’t a purchase—it’s a commission. You’re co-creating with someone who cares as much about your story as the craft itself. The process is a conversation, not a transaction.

Data Meets Artistry

The work begins with scientific precision—real elevation data, accurate contours—and is finished by hand. Precision carving preserves fidelity; wood selection, resin color, and finishing bring warmth and presence.

Heirloom Quality

These are legacy works—built to outlast trends, withstand moves, and carry meaning across generations. Archive-quality materials and time-tested joinery keep the work stable for the long life ahead.

Studio Commitments

Sustainable Sourcing

I source domestic hardwoods from certified sustainable forestry operations and architectural reclamation yards. Every piece of walnut, cherry, or maple has a story before it becomes yours.

Guided Process

From initial consultation to final crating, you're part of the journey. Materials, milestones, and decisions remain clear throughout.

No Mass Production

I keep production intentionally limited to protect quality. Each year, I accept only as many commissions as I can craft with full attention—start to finish. Quality over quantity, always.

Lifetime Support

Every piece includes care guidance and my direct contact. If the work is ever damaged—whether from transit, impact, or environmental factors—I'm available to assess and restore it when possible. These are built to endure.
