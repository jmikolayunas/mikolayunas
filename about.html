About

Meditations on place and permanence.

Every landscape holds its own record — compressed in rock strata, carved by water, written in the depth of a seafloor. I make work that tries to read it.

The materials have their own logic. Hardwood carries the grain of decades; each board is a cross-section of time before it becomes part of something else. Resin responds to depth the way water does: where it thickens, less light escapes. The color is not applied. It is depth itself, made visible.

What compels me are the paused forces — the evidence of something too large and too slow to witness directly. A coastline shaped by a billion tides. A mountain range still moving. A seafloor revealed and submerged twice daily by the largest tidal range on earth. They are portraits of time.

Each piece is unique.

Approach

Place as Identity

Geography isn’t just scenery. It’s where memory lives. The landscapes that shape us deserve to be held as art, never reduced to souvenirs or screens.

One-of-One

Every piece is made individually for one client, from one place. Each commission is approached with equal care for the geography itself and the attention required to honor it. The relationship is personal and deliberate.
