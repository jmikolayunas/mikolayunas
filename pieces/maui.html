"The Valley Isle"

Maui, Hawaii

← Back to Gallery
Materials: koa, birch, resin Dimensions: 29" × 24" × 3" Location: Maui, Hawaii Completed: March 2026

Relief of Maui: Hawaiian island formed by two shield volcanoes rising from the Pacific floor. Native koa wood embodies the island's dual volcanic peaks and connecting valley. Epoxy surrounds Maui with Pacific waters and the channels to neighboring islands.

Complete topographic composition

Detail of Mauna Kahālāwai

Angled installation view

A close circle of friends commissioned this piece as a wedding gift for a couple who make Maui home. The choice of native koa wood was about honoring what the island holds for all of them: the experiences and memories that have accumulated across visits and years. The material had to be from the place itself, the wood that grows where their collective story lives.

Koa was sourced from a Hawaiian mill specializing in sustainable harvest and cultural stewardship. The wood's character (tight grain with natural chatoyance) shimmers the reflected light, accentuating depth across the carved terrain. Epoxy was cast frameless, allowing the island to preserve its relationship with the ocean, refusing to frame what cannot be held. The work honors both the place and the friendships that brought it into being.

— Artist's Note
