Cannon Mountain

Franconia, New Hampshire

← Back to Gallery
Materials: maple, walnut Dimensions: 24" × 24" × 6½" Location: Cannon Mountain, Franconia, NH Completed: July 2025

Relief of Cannon Mountain, a steep White Mountain face rising hard above Franconia Notch. Hardwood carries the mountain's exposed ridges, abrupt transitions, and long fall lines. Laser-etched trail cuts move through the surface with restraint, keeping the weight of the mountain primary.

Complete topographic composition

Relief depth from ridgeline to valley

Approach to base terrain

Inverse relief detail

Created for a friend who patrols Cannon, watching weather, terrain, and people. For him, the mountain is not recreation; it is responsibility. That working relationship shaped how the piece needed to be made.

The carving was kept direct. Cannon's verticality and exposure were rendered without softening, with hard maple chosen for horizontal grain. The tree ring lines act almost like topographic contours, helping the terrain read from ridge to base.

Laser-etched treelines define ski trails as negative space, keeping the forest as detail rather than subject. The mountain stays primary: steep, technical, and uncompromising.

— Artist's Note
