Cape Ann

Massachusetts

← Back to Gallery
Materials: maple, birch, mahogany, resin Dimensions: 31" × 30" × 1½" Location: Cape Ann, Massachusetts Completed: November 2025

Topographic and bathymetric relief where granite coastline meets Atlantic shelf. Carved hardwood forms land and nearshore contours. Deep epoxy pour reveals submerged terrain. Laser-etched tributaries extend inland, connecting watershed to sea.

Full piece showing entire topography

Detail of coastal relief

Side angle showing depth

Fifty years. Same coastline, different names for the same rocks. This piece was for an anniversary—two people, one place, generations of return.

We worked to show continuity: how rivers feed the coast, how offshore contours shape the working water. The resin was mixed to feel grounded, not decorative. The result isn't just shoreline. It's the full system—land, water, memory, and the pull that keeps people coming back.

— Artist's Note
