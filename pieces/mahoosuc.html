Mahoosuc Range

Newry, Maine

← Back to Gallery
Materials: basswood, birch, walnut Dimensions: 22" × 33" × 4½" Location: Mahoosuc Range, Sunday River, Newry, Maine Completed: November 2024

Relief map capturing Sunday River's multi-peak structure and connected ridge system. Carved hardwood emphasizes terrain continuity across summits. Laser-etched trail network spans the range with clear readability. Tonal elevation shifts reveal how the mountain system links together.

Detail of carved terrain revealing wood grain and elevation transitions

Side angle showing dimensional depth of the mountain system

Low-angle lighting demonstrating how shadows reveal terrain elevation

Mahoosuc Range isn't one peak—it's a sequence. Movement, not arrival. Ridges link, valleys separate, trails braid across exposures.

The carving needed to express that flow: how the system connects, how you move through it rather than up it. Trail etching was designed for clarity at distance, so the network reads as possibility. The deeper structure—carved terrain—holds the invitation: this is a place built for range, not a single summit.

— Artist's Note
