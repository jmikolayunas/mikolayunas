Mount Okemo

Ludlow, Vermont

← Back to Gallery
Materials: maple, walnut Dimensions: 23" × 26" × 5½" Location: Mount Okemo, Ludlow, Vermont Completed: November 2023

Relief carving of Mount Okemo with laser-etched ski trail network across multi-peak terrain. Carved hardwood reveals broad face and varied exposures. Graduated tones emphasize base-to-summit transitions. Trail etching spans the full system—greens converging below, blues threading mid-mountain, blacks carving the steeps.

This piece moved between families, not individuals. A gift honoring the mountain where two households converge, year after year. Okemo holds accumulated time: kids learning to ski, long weekends, school breaks, the rhythm of separate lives overlapping on shared terrain.

We carved to show full scope—base to peak, beginner to expert—because that range is the story. Families spread across the mountain but reconvene. The result isn't a map of Okemo. It's a record of what the mountain makes possible: separate families becoming one extended crew, bound by snow and repetition.

— Artist's Note
