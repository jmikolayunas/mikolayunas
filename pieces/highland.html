Highland Mountain

Northfield, New Hampshire

← Back to Gallery
Materials: maple, walnut Dimensions: 24" × 24" × 5½" Location: Highland Mountain, Northfield, NH Completed: November 2023

Relief carving of Highland Mountain Bike Park emphasizing the flow trails and technical terrain that define this lift-served network. Laser-etched trail system preserves the intricate routing of New England's premier gravity park. Wood tones reveal the mountain's pitch and character.

View of terrain elevation

Back side relief

Detail of trail network

A rider who knows Highland inside and out commissioned this piece for the friend who dialed in the trails alongside him. This wasn't about documentation—it was about shared ground. Highland is where they return—not for novelty, but for rhythm. Shared lines, repeated descents, the kind of riding that doesn't need explanation.

During consultation, the focus was clarity: every trail needed to read, from mellow flow to steep technical. The result is a portrait of shared ground—not just where they ride, but what binds them. Lift laps, progression, the way a place becomes shorthand for friendship.

— Artist's Note
