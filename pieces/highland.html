Highland Mountain

Northfield, New Hampshire

← Back to Gallery
Materials: maple, walnut Dimensions: 24" × 24" × 5½" Location: Highland Mountain, Northfield, NH Completed: December 2025

Relief of Highland Mountain rising from New Hampshire's granite bedrock. Hardwood captures the technical terrain and purpose-built features across varied pitch. Laser-etched trail system maps the intricate routing from flow zones to steep technical descents.

A Highland veteran commissioned this piece for the friend who mastered its lines alongside him. This was about shared ground. Highland is where they return for rhythm: repeated descents, the kind of riding that doesn't need explanation.

During consultation, the focus was clarity: every trail needed to read distinctly, from mellow flow to steep technical. Strategic vertical exaggeration accentuates the rugged physicality of the terrain, the epitome of "East Coast" riding. The laser-etched network preserves the intricate trail connections, where features stack, where berms redirect. Lift laps, progression, the way a place becomes shorthand for friendship.

— Artist's Note
