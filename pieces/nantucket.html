Nantucket Island

Massachusetts

← Back to Gallery
Materials: maple, birch, walnut, resin Dimensions: 20" × 32" × 1½" Location: Nantucket Island, Massachusetts Completed: November 2023

Topographic and bathymetric relief emphasizing Nantucket's shifting edge. Carved hardwood captures subtle inland elevations. Layered epoxy reveals sandbars, tidal ripples, and nearshore movement patterns. The piece reads as formation in progress—sand, current, time.

Complete topographic composition

Detail of coastal relief

Angled installation view showing dimensional depth

Close view of wood grain

Nantucket is made by process, not permanence. Tides carve channels. Sandbars appear, dissolve, reappear elsewhere. Currents shape what looks stable from above.

This piece prioritizes that truth. The water’s transparency grants witness to those unseen forces. It's evidence: how an island is made, and remade, continuously.

— Artist's Note
