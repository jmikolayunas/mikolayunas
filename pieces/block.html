Block Island

Rhode Island

← Back to Gallery
Materials: maple, birch, mahogany, resin Dimensions: 27" × 32" × 1¾" Location: Block Island, Rhode Island Completed: June 2025

Relief of Block Island, a glacial island set between Rhode Island Sound and the open Atlantic. Hardwood defines the island's compact interior, clay bluffs, and surrounding coastal edge. Translucent resin carries the bathymetry outward, revealing the northern ebb shoal and the shelf falling away to the south.

Complete topographic composition

Detail of coastal relief

Angled installation view

This piece began with a wedding at Block Island's southern Mohegan Bluffs, where clay cliffs drop sharply toward the Atlantic. For the couple, the island is not a single memory but a place of return: summer rides between harbors, familiar roads, and ten square miles that continue to feel vast.

The carving uses vertical exaggeration to give the island's modest terrain its emotional scale. The southern bluffs receive special emphasis in the relief, not as a labeled landmark, but as the place where the story gathers weight.

The resin was mixed toward the color of coastal summer water: a sea-glass green with life beneath it. Land, water, and memory hold together here: an island made intimate through return.

— Artist's Note
