Block Island

Rhode Island

← Back to Gallery
Materials: maple, birch, mahogany, resin Dimensions: 27" × 32" × 1¾" Location: Block Island, Rhode Island Completed: November 2024

Topographic and bathymetric relief of Block Island—ten square miles where New England bedrock meets open Atlantic. Carved hardwood captures the island's rolling interior and dramatic coastal bluffs. Deep epoxy pour reveals surrounding seafloor: tidal channels to the north, sand waves and glacial drift patterns, broad continental shelf extending south.

Summers only, but for decades. Two childhoods spent on the same small island, hundreds of miles from where they lived the rest of the year. When they married atop the Mohegan Bluffs, they wanted the place held in form.

During consultation, they spoke in landmarks: the clay cliffs on the southeast shore, the ride between harbors, the way ten square miles can feel vast. We carved the full island—compact in scale, but with vertical exaggeration to preserve the coastal drama that defines it.

The resin was color-matched from photographs they sent—their last visit, early morning light on calm water. The result isn't just accurate. It's theirs—geography as memory, place as return.

— Artist's Note
