Block Island

Rhode Island

← Back to Gallery
Materials: maple, birch, mahogany, resin Dimensions: 27" × 32" × 1¾" Location: Block Island, Rhode Island Completed: June 2025

Relief of Block Island—ten square miles where New England bedrock meets open Atlantic. Hardwood defines the island's interior and dramatic coastal bluffs. Epoxy exposes seafloor features: its distinctive northern ebb shoal and the continental shelf extending south.

Commissioned to commemorate being wed at Block Island's southern Mohegan Bluffs. The couple returns every summer, cycling around the island—ten square miles that feel vast. During consultation, they spoke in landmarks: the clay cliffs on the southeast shore, the ride between harbors, the way modest terrain holds meaning through return.

The island was carved with vertical exaggeration to emphasize it's rugged interior and preserve the coastal drama. The southern bluffs—where they were married—receive particular emphasis in the relief, showing how dramatically the land drops to Atlantic. Resin color was chosen to reflect the Atlantic's coastal summer waters: sea-glass green resulting from the abundance of life.

— Artist's Note
