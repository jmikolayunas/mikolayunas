# Bonas Studio — Brand Voice & Luxury Positioning

## Brand Philosophy
- Commission-based model: every piece requires client collaboration through an intentionally iterative process
- Gallery-style presentation: emphasize contemplation and breathing room over typical web conventions
- Slow visitors down: museum-quality experience prioritizing thoughtfulness over energy
- Exclusive atelier positioning: custom studio, not e-commerce
- Heirloom quality: decisions reinforce craftsmanship + legacy over mass production

## Psychology of Luxury Clientele
- Use confident, direct language (avoid defensive or apologetic phrasing)
- Avoid corporate justifications or excessive meta-commentary
- Position custom work as a collaborative creative partnership between equals
- Warm and accessible, but still exclusive
- Never apologize for pricing, timelines, or process requirements

Avoid phrases like:
- “I understand this may seem…”
- “Please note that…”
- “Going forward…”
- “At this time…”
- “Please be advised…”

## Tone Standards

✅ DO
- Confident assertions: “We transform terrain into heirloom artwork.”
- Collaboration framing: “Together, we refine…”, “You shape the vision.”
- Lead with value: “Precision-built, hand-finished, artist-guided.”
- Active voice: “I’ll guide you through…” (not passive)
- Warm + professional: “Let’s explore options.”

❌ DON’T
- Defensive language: “I understand this may seem expensive, but…”
- Apologize for process: “Please note revisions are limited…”
- Corporate jargon: “Going forward”, “At this time”
- Over-explain decisions
- Tentative phrasing: “I hope to…”, “We try to…”, “Ideally…”

## Avoiding “Investment” Language
- “Investment” can feel sales-y
- “Investment breakdown” can be acceptable in proposals
- Prefer: pricing, cost, commission range, or simply state the price
- Never use “investment in quality” justifications

## Artist-Guided Process Language (copy)
- “I’ll test options and share results” (not “you can request changes”)
- “We’ll refine at key milestones” (not “revisions available”)
- “Decisions evolve as the piece takes shape” (not “subject to approval”)

## Content Patterns

### Hero Messaging
- Primary tagline: “Where Landscape Becomes Legacy”
- Supporting line: “The art of place, handcrafted and captured in collaboration”
- Lead with emotion + meaning, follow with precision + craft

### Process Descriptions
- Balance technical precision with emotional resonance
- Structure: What happens → Why it matters → How it serves the story
- Prefer specifics: “12–36 hours of carving time” over vague phrasing
- Use sensory language and material detail

### Artist’s Notes (Individual Pieces)
- Format: gallery voice + personal narrative
- Structure: Context → Technical approach → Personal reflection
- Length: ~3–5 paragraphs, thoughtful + conversational
- Example opening: “This piece began as a question about scale…”

### Commission Copy
- Collaborative exploration, not a transaction
- Prefer: “Fill out the form to begin the conversation”
- Avoid: “Submit your inquiry”
- Prefer: “We’ll refine the design together until it feels right”
