In the Collection

Works placed in private homes and collections.

Collector's Note
We've summered on the Cape Ann shore since before the children were born. When I first saw it — the way the seafloor drops away just past the harbor — I understood immediately that this wasn't a map. It holds the place the way memory does. It lives above the fireplace now, and every person who walks into that room stops.

— Margaret, Gloucester, MA

Collector's Note
My husband skied Bromley for forty years. I commissioned this for his retirement and had it waiting when he got home. He stood there for a long time without saying anything. That was the whole point.

— Christine, Manchester, VT

Collector's Note
We did the Mahoosuc Notch together the summer we got engaged. This piece now hangs in our entry — the first thing anyone sees when they come through the door. It's a good way to start a conversation about what matters.

— R. & T., Portland, ME
