From Data to Heirloom

Every piece begins with a conversation and ends with a legacy. This is the journey from geographic data to handcrafted artwork—where precision meets artistry, and collaboration shapes creation.

01

Discovery

We Begin With Place

Every commission starts with a conversation—not about dimensions or deadlines, but about meaning. What place holds your story? A mountain you climbed with family? The coastline where you scattered ashes? The city where you fell in love?

During our initial consultation, I listen carefully. Your story guides every decision that follows—from the geographic bounds we'll capture to the materials that will honor the place's character.

I'll also show you examples at different scales, discuss materials, and explain how topographic data translates to physical relief. By the end of our call, you'll understand exactly what's possible.

  • 30-45 minute video consultation
  • Material samples and references shared
  • Initial feasibility assessment
02

Collaboration

You Shape the Vision

Within 2-3 days of our call, I'll send you a detailed proposal with digital mockups showing how your topography will translate to relief. You'll see the geographic area, vertical exaggeration, orientation, and scale.

This is where you become co-creator. Should we include surrounding terrain for context, or zoom tight on a specific feature? Cherry with dark finish, or walnut with natural oil? Deep blue resin for water, or subtle translucent pour?

We'll refine the design together until it feels right—not just accurate, but emotionally resonant. You approve the final design before I acquire materials or begin fabrication.

  • 3D digital mockups with accurate topography
  • Material and finish options presented
  • Detailed timeline and investment breakdown
  • Revisions included until you're satisfied
03

Data Crafting

Science Becomes Art

Once you approve the design and deposit is received, I begin the technical work. I source high-resolution elevation data from USGS, NOAA, or international datasets— sometimes merging multiple sources for optimal detail.

This raw data is processed, cleaned, and translated into toolpaths for CNC (computer numerical control) machining. I adjust vertical exaggeration to ensure features read clearly at your chosen scale, and optimize cutting strategies to preserve fine detail while maintaining structural integrity.

Meanwhile, I'm sourcing your wood. Every board is hand-selected for grain pattern, color consistency, and figure. Sustainable domestic hardwoods from certified mills and salvage yards—each piece with its own story before it becomes yours.

  • Geospatial data acquisition and processing
  • 3D model optimization for fabrication
  • Hand-selected wood sourcing
  • CNC toolpath generation
04

Fabrication

Machine Precision, Human Touch

The CNC router carves your topography with sub-millimeter precision over 8-12 hours. I monitor the entire process, adjusting feeds and speeds to prevent tearout and preserve the finest contour details. What emerges is raw geography—accurate, but not yet art.

Next, I laser-etch the surface with a custom map overlay— trail lines, tree lines, waterway extensions, or locations of your choosing directly into the grain with crisp detail. It’s a quiet layer of storytelling that locks the design to the land before any finish or resin goes down.

Then the hand work begins. I sand progressively through multiple grits, following the terrain's natural flow. Edges are chamfered or left raw depending on the aesthetic. Finish is applied—oil, stain, or charcoal treatment—to enhance grain and add depth.

If your piece includes water features, I mix and pour epoxy resin in custom colors— multiple thin layers for clarity and depth. Brass inlay is cut and set by hand for summit markers, coastlines, or personal coordinates. Every detail is intentional.

  • 8-12 hours of precision CNC carving
  • 10-16 hours of precision CNC laser etching (if applicable)
  • Hand sanding and finish application
  • Resin pouring in custom colors (if applicable)
  • Brass inlay and detail work
  • Progress photos sent throughout
05

Heirloom

Delivered, Installed, Cherished

Your finished piece is photographed, signed on the back with details of location and creation date, and sealed with a final protective coat. I build a custom crate with foam padding and corner bracing—these pieces ship like the art they are.

Delivery is via specialized art carrier with full insurance and tracking. Local clients can arrange studio pickup, and I'm available for installation consultation—proper mounting is crucial for pieces of this weight and scale.

But delivery isn't the end of our relationship. Every piece comes with care instructions and my direct contact. Years later, if you need repair, refinishing, or even a companion piece, I'm here. These aren't products—they're heirlooms meant to outlive us both.

  • Professional photography and documentation
  • Custom crating and packaging
  • White-glove art carrier shipping
  • Installation guidance provided
  • Lifetime support and restoration services
