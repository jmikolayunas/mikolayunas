# Website Flow - Beginning-of-Sentence Redundancy Analysis & Edit Plan

## VIEWING THE SITE AS ONE CONTINUOUS NARRATIVE

Reading from Home → Gallery → Process → Commission → About → Contact as a single story.

---

## PATTERN 1: "THE + NOUN" OPENINGS (HIGH FREQUENCY)

### Issue: Too many sentences start with "The [work/piece/process/design/carving/goal/focus]"

**Found in:**
- About page: "The work investigates..." / "The work gives physical form..."
- Process page (multiple stages): "The model displays..." / "The goal is..." / "The process concludes..." / "The carving was shaped..."
- Commission page: "The process is guided..." / "The aim is transformation..."
- Piece artist's notes: "The piece was designed..." / "The carving was shaped..." / "The design was built..."

---

### RECOMMENDED EDITS:

**ABOUT PAGE - P1 (about.html, line 72)**
```
CURRENT: The work investigates the relationship between identity and place
FIX: Explores the relationship between identity and place
REASON: More active, varies from "I create" opening
```

**ABOUT PAGE - P3 (about.html, line 80)**
```
CURRENT: The work gives physical form to terrain through carving, material, finish, and frame.
FIX: Physical form emerges through carving, material, finish, and frame.
REASON: More dynamic, varies from "The work" repetition
ALTERNATIVE: Carving, material, finish, and frame give physical form to terrain.
```

**PROCESS - Stage 01 (process.html, ~line 92)**
```
CURRENT: The conversation guides the creative direction without dictating it.
FIX: This conversation guides creative direction without dictating it.
REASON: "This" creates reference to previous sentence flow
ALTERNATIVE: Guidance emerges from conversation, not prescription.
```

**PROCESS - Stage 02 (process.html, ~line 129)**
```
CURRENT: The model displays geographic scope, vertical exaggeration, orientation, and scale
FIX: It displays geographic scope, vertical exaggeration, orientation, and scale
REASON: "It" refers back to "3D viewer" naturally, avoids "The model"
ALTERNATIVE: Geographic scope, vertical exaggeration, orientation, and scale are all visible
```

**PROCESS - Stage 02 (process.html, ~line 134)**
```
CURRENT: The goal is always the same: a piece that feels inevitable, not forced.
FIX: Always the same goal: a piece that feels inevitable, not forced.
REASON: Inverted structure, more confident
ALTERNATIVE: Every commission aims for the same outcome: inevitable, not forced.
```

## PATTERN 3: PASSIVE "WAS + VERB" CONSTRUCTIONS

### Issue: Too many sentences use passive voice with past tense auxiliary

**Found in:**
- Process Stage 04: "The carving was shaped..." / "Resin was cast..." / "Vertical exaggeration was used..."
- Piece artist's notes: "The piece was designed..." / "The design was built..." / "Koa was sourced..."

---

### RECOMMENDED EDITS:

**PROCESS - Stage 04 (Fabrication paragraph - various lines)**
```
CURRENT: Along the way, I test options—finish tone, resin depth, overlay weight.
CURRENT: Depending on the commission, these choices are made collaboratively or guided by artistic judgment to honor the place.
FIX: Options get tested as the piece develops: finish tone, resin depth, overlay weight. Depending on the commission, these choices emerge collaboratively or through artistic judgment.
REASON: More active, connects testing to development, "emerge" vs passive "are made"
```

## PATTERN 4: REPEATED "DURING/ALONG THE WAY/AT KEY POINTS" TEMPORAL MARKERS

### Issue: Similar transitional phrases throughout Process page

**Found in Process page:**
- "During the initial consultation..."
- "Along the way, I test options..."
- "At key milestones..."

**PROCESS - Stage 04 (process.html)**

## BEFORE & AFTER - KEY SECTIONS

### ABOUT PAGE - ARTIST STATEMENT

**BEFORE:**
```
I create dimensional relief sculptures from real terrain data. 
Each piece begins with a specific geography and explores it through commission.
The work investigates the relationship between identity and place, and how the landscapes we return to become part of who we are.

What sustains the work is that each place asks something new.
Different coastlines, mountain systems, trail networks, and scales demand different solutions. 
With every commission, the craft advances toward a deeper understanding of how a place can be held through art.

The work gives physical form to terrain through carving, material, finish, and frame. 
Wood species, resin tone, and surface treatment are resolved in response to the land itself and the story it carries.
Each piece is made for one place and one story at a time, built to be lived with, held onto, and passed on.
```

**AFTER:**
```
I create dimensional relief sculptures from real terrain data. 
A specific geography, explored through commission.
Explores the relationship between identity and place, and how the landscapes we return to become part of who we are.

Each place asks something new.
Different coastlines, mountain systems, trail networks, and scales demand different solutions. 
With every commission, the craft advances toward a deeper understanding of how a place can be held through art.

Physical form emerges through carving, material, finish, and frame. 
Wood species, resin tone, and surface treatment are resolved in response to the land itself and the story it carries.
One place and one story at a time, built to be lived with, held onto, and passed on.
```

**IMPROVEMENTS:**
- Eliminates 3 uses of "The work"
- Eliminates "What sustains" construction
- Eliminates 2 uses of "Each piece"
- Creates more varied sentence rhythm
- Fragments add punch without losing clarity
- More active voice throughout
